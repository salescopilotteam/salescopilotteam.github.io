CardKeep (“the app”) is built to keep card information on your device only. This policy explains what the app actually handles and where.

1. What the operator collects

The operator does not collect or store on any server the personal data of users or the data printed on their cards. There is no sign-up and no login, and there are no advertising or analytics SDKs. Payment happens only through the app store, and the operator never receives payment details.

2. What is stored on the device

Original card photos, names, companies, job titles, phone numbers, emails, notes, labels, places, reactions, important marks and follow-up appointments are stored only in the app’s own storage on your device.

Usage counts (for example how many cards were saved) record only an event name and a time, never linked to card contents. You can read and clear them in Settings. They are never sent anywhere.

3. When data leaves the device

Data leaves the device only when you choose to send it: sharing card details, sharing a photo, exporting CSV or vCard, saving a backup file, sharing an iCalendar file, opening the phone, messaging, mail or maps app, and adding an event to your calendar.

You pick the receiving app or service in the operating system share sheet, and what happens after that follows that app or service’s own policy.

4. Text recognition and Google ML Kit

Card text is recognised by Google ML Kit running on the device. Card images and recognition results are not uploaded to an OCR server.

ML Kit may however contact Google servers for model and compatibility updates and for performance and usage metrics. Those requests do not include card images or the recognised text.

5. Permissions

Camera: requested when you take a photo of a card.

Photos: requested when you choose a card photo from your album.

Calendar: on iPhone, write-only access is requested when you add an event. On Android no calendar permission is requested; a calendar file is shared instead.

Contacts, call logs and the microphone are never requested.

6. Other people’s information on a card

A business card is usually someone else’s personal data. Keep it for the purpose it was given to you, and check the recipient and the service before you export or share it.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it. Deleting from a card removes that card and its photo; “Delete all cards and photos” in Settings removes everything. Uninstalling the app also removes the app’s storage, as the operating system deletes it.

The operator keeps no server copy, so data deleted from the device cannot be recovered for you.

8. Your rights

You can read, edit, export and delete your data inside the app at any time. These are not turned into paid features.

9. Changes to this policy

If a feature that changes how data is handled is added, such as login or cloud backup, this policy is updated and announced inside the app before it takes effect.
