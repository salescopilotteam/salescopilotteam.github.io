Saving a card

Tap the camera button at the bottom right of your library. The original photo is kept whole, without cropping, and the text is read on the device to fill in the name, company, job title, phone and email.

A card is saved even if you do not know the name. Open a photo-only card later to fill in the details.

For cards already in your album, use “From album” or “Several” to import up to 15 at a time.

Finding a card

Type part of a name, company, job title, phone number, email, note, label or place into the search box and the list narrows straight away.

Choosing several labels keeps only the cards that have all of them. Once a filter is on, the labels and places used alongside it appear in the results, and tapping one adds it to the filter.

Labels and places

A card can carry several labels but only one place. The edit screen suggests values you already use; tap one to apply it.

Tap “Filter” above the library to open the full list of labels and places. The “+” at the end of a row creates a new one, and you can search for cards and apply it to many at once.

Long press to edit

Long press a label or place chip to open its menu: pick and edit cards, change the colour (labels only), rename or delete.

Renaming and deleting apply to every card carrying that value. Deleting the value does not delete the cards.

Important people and label colours

Turn on “Important” next to the name on a card to pin it to the top of the library with a star, and use “Important” in the filter to see them together.

A label can take one of six colours. Labels without a colour keep the plain style.

Getting in touch and calendars

Tapping call, message or email opens the app on your device with the number or address already filled in. This app never sends anything on your behalf.

“Add to calendar” and “Share as iCalendar” create a follow-up appointment that you confirm yourself before it is saved.

Save limit

You can save up to 30 cards for free. The limit counts how many cards you have saved, so deleting a card does not free up a slot.

Once you reach the limit, the cards you already have can still be opened, searched, edited, exported and deleted. Only saving new ones stops.

Exporting and deleting

Settings can export your cards as CSV or vCard. Photos and reaction emoji are not included in the file.

“Delete all cards and photos” in Settings cannot be undone, so export first.

Where your data lives

Card photos and everything you type stay on this device. They go away if you delete the app or reset the device, so make a backup or an export before changing devices.
