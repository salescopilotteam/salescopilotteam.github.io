These terms set out the conditions for using CardKeep.

1. The service

The app is a tool for photographing and keeping the cards you receive, and for organising them with labels, places and notes so you can find them again. There are no ads, no sign-up and no login.

2. Responsibility for your data

Card data is not stored on CardKeep operator servers. If you enable Android Google Backup or iPhone iCloud device backup, the operating system may include app data in the backup for your account. Data can be lost if the device is lost, breaks or is reset, or if you delete the app, so keep your own copy with an encrypted backup or an export when you need one.

3. Save limit

You can save up to 30 cards for free.

The limit is based on how many cards you have saved so far. A new card counts as one whether you made it from a photo or typed it in. Deleting a card does not reduce the count you have already used.

To save more than the free limit, you may buy a one-time unlimited-save product through the Google Play or Apple store checkout shown in the app. The store shows the local price. The limit is removed only after the purchase is confirmed; viewing, editing, exporting and deleting existing cards remain available without payment.

The limit applies only to saving new cards. Viewing, searching, editing, exporting and deleting the cards you already have is always available.

4. Other people’s information

You must use the information on other people’s cards only within the purpose for which it was given to you, and never for unlawful purposes such as unsolicited marketing or resale.

5. Limits of text recognition

Automatically recognised text can be wrong. Check it against the original photo before you get in touch.

6. Limitation of liability

The operator works to provide the service reliably, but features may change and errors may occur. To the extent permitted by law, liability for data loss or indirect damage is limited.

7. Changes to these terms

If the conditions of use change, for example because accounts or cloud features are added, this is announced inside the app before it takes effect.

The free save limit is 30 cards for every user. If the limit needs to change, we will announce it inside the app before it takes effect.
