1. 100% On-Device (Zero-Server) Architecture

Warranty Copilot is designed with a strict privacy-first architecture. All warranty photos, receipt images, model numbers, purchase dates, stores, and notes are processed and stored exclusively on your device. We do not operate external servers, and your data is never uploaded to the cloud.

2. Camera & Photo Library Access

Camera and Photo permissions are used solely for capturing documents and performing on-device optical character recognition (OCR). No images are ever sent to remote AI services.

3. Advertising and your choices

The free, ad-supported edition distributed through the Korean Google Play storefront may display banner ads through Google Mobile Ads. Google may process device identifiers, IP address, and ad interaction data under its privacy policy. Where required, we show Google’s consent form before requesting ads; if consent cannot be obtained, we do not request ads. Warranty images and in-app warranty data are never shared for advertising.

4. AES-256 Encrypted Backups

Manual backups are encrypted using military-grade AES-256-GCM with your personal passphrase. Only you can decrypt your backup file.
